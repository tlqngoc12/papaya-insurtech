## Definitions

- A claim is **predicted fraud** when `risk_score >= T`.
- **Precision** = TP / (TP + FP). **Recall** = TP / (TP + FN). **F1** = 2PR / (P + R).
- **FPR (standard)** = FP / (FP + TN): false positives over all legit claims. This is the primary reading of the challenge target (M1).
- **FDR** = FP / (TP + FP) = 1 - precision: false positives over flagged claims. Reported because the challenge's "false positive rate" can also be read this way.
- **Recall by pattern**: claims of a fraud pattern that score at least T. *Flagged by own rule* counts the claims of that pattern that carry the rule of the same name, whatever their score.
- **Precision by rule**: over the claims carrying a flag of the rule (whatever their score), the share that is fraud of any pattern, and the share that is fraud of the pattern of the same name.
- **precision@k**: share of fraud among the top k claims of a ranking.
